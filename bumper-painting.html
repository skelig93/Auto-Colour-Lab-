Bumper painting and plastic refinishing

Bumper painting in Auckland needs the right approach for plastic: flexible materials, correct prep and colour that matches adjacent panels. Auto Paint Lab refinishes scuffed, scratched or poorly matched bumpers so they don’t stand out from the rest of the vehicle.

Typical bumper paint jobs

  • Parking scuffs and scrapes on front or rear bumpers
  • Paint transfer and surface abrasion
  • Partial or full bumper respray after repair
  • Colour matching to guards, doors and factory-style finishes
  • Plastic bumper repair assessment before paint (where structural clips allow)

Why bumpers need specialist prep

Plastic expands and flexes differently to metal. Cutting corners on adhesion or flexibility shows up as peeling or cracking later. We prepare and paint bumpers for durability as well as appearance — useful whether you need a full bumper paint or a localised scuff repair.

What we do on site

Touch-ups and repairs where your car is parked.

Mobile paint work is ideal for smaller clear coat and colour coat repairs, localised scratches and many accident-related paint touch-ups that do not need a full workshop bay.

Clear coat touch-ups

Localised clear coat repair and blending where the clear has been marked, dull or compromised, without unnecessary full-panel work.

Colour coat repairs

Colour coat touch-ups and blending for chips, scratches and small damaged areas so the repair sits cleanly with the surrounding paint.

Scratch repairs

Assessment and repair of scratches — from light scuffs to deeper marks — with the right preparation and finish for a durable result.

Accident paint repairs

On-site paint repair for suitable accident damage where the panel work allows a mobile finish rather than a full off-site respray.

Larger resprays and bigger jobs are completed off site at the workshop. Mobile visits focus on practical on-site touch-ups and repairs.

Mobile paint support vehicle

We come to the vehicle.

Work is carried out on site using our mobile paint support vehicle. This is not a vehicle transporter — it carries the tools, materials and equipment needed for mobile paint support.

Requirements

What we need on site.

To complete mobile paint work safely and cleanly, a few practical conditions need to be met.

Site requirements

  • Parking space — there must be space for our vehicle to park next to the vehicle being worked on.
  • Access — safe, practical access to the panels being repaired (driveway, carpark or similar).
  • Power — where power is used from the property, it is metered. That power cost is measured and removed from your bill before payment.
  • Suitable conditions — weather and surface conditions must allow a proper mobile finish. If conditions are not right, we will say so before starting.
No payment until the job is finished and you are happy.

You are not asked to pay before the work is complete. Payment is due once the agreed job is finished and you are satisfied with the result.

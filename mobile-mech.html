Mobile Automotive Services

What Mobile Mech provides.

Mobile Mech provides practical automotive support for private vehicle owners, tradespeople and businesses across Auckland. Services cover routine maintenance, repairs, diagnostics, electrical fault finding and specialist vehicle checks.

01

Car Servicing

Routine servicing and maintenance carried out at home or work where suitable, helping reduce the disruption of taking the vehicle to a workshop.

02

Mobile Mechanical Repairs

Brake and suspension work, starting problems, cooling-system issues, batteries and other mechanical faults where the repair is suitable for a mobile service.

03

Vehicle Diagnostics

Diagnostic scanning and fault finding for warning lights, drivability problems, electrical faults, starting issues and intermittent vehicle faults.

04

Auto Electrical

Battery, charging, starting, sensors, warning lights and electrical circuit fault finding using diagnostic equipment and testing.

05

Pre-Purchase Inspections

Vehicle inspections covering visible condition, mechanical concerns, warning lights and diagnostic information where suitable.

06

EV Battery Service

EV and plug-in hybrid battery state-of-health testing and battery service support.

07

Tyres & Wheel Alignment

Tyre replacement, balancing and wheel-alignment support, with related tyre wear and steering concerns assessed.

08

Headlight Restoration

Restoration of cloudy, faded and yellowed plastic headlight lenses.

09

Fleet Servicing

Mobile servicing and maintenance for Auckland businesses running two or more vehicles, with scheduled service and fleet support.

10

Oil Leak Repair

Inspection, diagnosis and repair advice for visible oil leaks, oil smells, gaskets, seals and engine oil leak sources.

11

Engine Check Light

Fault-code scanning and diagnosis to help identify the likely cause of an engine warning light and determine the appropriate next step.

12

Windscreen Replacement

Windscreen replacement support for cracked, chipped or damaged vehicle glass, with the correct replacement confirmed for the vehicle.
